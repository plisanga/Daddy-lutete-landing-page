About

Daddy Lutete is an international investments specialist focused on Africa. He owns Royal Golden LLC, an African-based company headquartered in New York State, and is a principal partner at Afro Exchange Pro. With his network and knowledge of the African market, he acts as a bridge between global investors and public and private projects across the continent, so that capital reaches the places where development and growth are needed most.

He holds a licence in mathematics, computer science and telecommunications, with further training in finance, marketing and business management. Since 2014 he has also volunteered in public relations and internal control with PROCCUDE, a disaster and humanitarian relief organization.
